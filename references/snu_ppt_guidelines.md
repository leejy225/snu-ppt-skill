# SNU PPT Guidelines

## Overview

Create a 16:9 presentation deck using the Seoul National University visual identity. The deck should feel institutional, precise, academic, and modern: clean white space, disciplined grid alignment, strong hierarchy, and restrained use of official SNU colors.

The visual system is built around SNU Blue as the primary identity color, supported by SNU Beige, SNU Gray, SNU Gold, and SNU Silver. Use white or very light backgrounds for most content slides, with SNU Blue reserved for headers, section markers, key emphasis, divider rules, and selected full-color title or chapter slides.

Every slide must maintain a consistent academic layout system. Chapter name, slide title, and subtitle should appear in the same position across slides whenever the slide type allows. Avoid drifting headers or inconsistent title placement between pages.

The deck must never feel empty at the bottom. Use the lower region of each slide intelligently with supporting notes, footnotes, source lines, compact callouts, visual anchors, data labels, small diagrams, summary rows, or structured content blocks, while preserving clarity and elegance.

## Slide Format

- Output only 16:9 slides.
- Use a consistent widescreen layout across the entire deck.
- Keep margins stable across all slides.
- Prefer structured, dense-but-readable layouts over sparse one-line slides.
- Do not create 4:3 slides or any non-widescreen format.

## Brand Assets

### Bottom-Right SNU Logo

Use `assets/snu_ui_download.png` as the official Seoul National University logo reference.

- Place the SNU logo at the bottom-right of each slide whenever possible.
- The logo must be aligned to the slide grid and should not float randomly.
- Minimum rendered size: at least 10 mm wide and 10 mm tall.
- Preserve the logo's original proportions.
- Keep adequate clear space around the logo.
- Do not distort, recolor, crop, rotate, or add effects to the logo.

### Top-Right 80th Anniversary Emblem

Use `assets/Basic Horizontal with SNU Emblem KR Navy.png` as the Seoul National University 80th anniversary emblem.

- Place the 80th anniversary emblem at the top-right of each slide where appropriate.
- It should be slightly larger than the bottom-right SNU logo.
- Maintain its original 16:9 aspect ratio.
- Keep it aligned with the title/header grid.
- Do not stretch, recolor, crop, rotate, or add visual effects.
- Avoid competing with the main slide title; it should read as a formal institutional mark.

### Typography Rules

- Use Yoon Gothic for all Korean body, titles, subtitles, labels, and captions.
- Use Univers LT Pro for English words, acronyms, numbers, dates, units, and chart labels.
- Keep typography clean, formal, and academic.
- Use medium weights for titles and regular weights for body text.
- Avoid overly decorative, handwritten, condensed, or playful typefaces.
- Maintain consistent title, subtitle, chapter, body, caption, and footnote styles across the deck.

### Suggested Hierarchy

- Chapter label: small, uppercase or compact Korean label, SNU Blue or SNU Gray
- Main slide title: strong Yoon Gothic / Univers, consistent top-left position
- Subtitle: smaller, SNU Gray, directly below title
- Body text: clear and compact, no excessive line spacing
- Captions / sources: small but readable, preferably bottom-left or aligned with content grid
- Numbers and data labels: Univers LT Pro, clean and tabular where possible

## Layout System

Every slide should follow a stable institutional grid.

### Header Placement

Keep these elements in consistent positions:

- Chapter name: upper-left, above or beside the main title
- Slide title: upper-left, fixed across slides
- Subtitle: directly below title
- 80th anniversary emblem: upper-right
- SNU logo: lower-right

### Content Area

- Use the central and lower slide regions actively.
- Avoid leaving the bottom third empty unless the slide is a deliberate title or transition page.
- Fill content areas with structured layouts such as:
  - two-column explanation + visual
  - chart + key takeaway
  - table + commentary
  - timeline + summary
  - diagram + notes
  - comparison grid
  - dense executive-summary blocks
- Keep all content aligned to the same grid.
- Do not scatter objects freely.

### Footer

- Use the footer area for source notes, slide numbers, compact summary statements, or institutional marks.
- Keep the SNU logo bottom-right.
- Source text may sit bottom-left or above the footer baseline.
- Footer elements should be subtle and should not compete with the main content.

## Slide Types

### Title Slide

- Large main title.
- Subtitle or presentation context below.
- 80th anniversary emblem top-right.
- SNU logo bottom-right.
- Use SNU Blue as either a full-width band, side panel, or strong typographic accent.
- Keep the composition formal and prestigious.

### Chapter Divider

- Use a strong SNU Blue field, vertical bar, or large chapter number.
- Include chapter name in a fixed position.
- Keep the 80th anniversary emblem and SNU logo visible if they do not overcrowd the slide.
- Minimal but not empty.

### Standard Content Slide

- Chapter label, title, and subtitle stay in fixed top-left positions.
- Main content occupies the middle and lower regions.
- Use compact but readable information density.
- Include bottom notes, sources, or summary strips when useful.
- SNU logo remains bottom-right.

### Data Slide

- Use clear chart hierarchy.
- Prefer SNU Blue for the primary data series.
- Use SNU Gray, SNU Beige, Gold, or Silver for secondary series.
- Label charts directly where possible.
- Include a concise takeaway near the chart, not buried in prose.
- Keep chart axes and gridlines light.

### Summary Slide

- Use structured blocks, numbered insights, or conclusion rows.
- Avoid a single sparse paragraph.
- Use the lower slide area for implications, action items, or next steps.

## Visual Style

- Formal, academic, and modern.
- High clarity over decoration.
- Minimal shadows.
- Thin rules, clean dividers, and disciplined alignment.
- Use SNU Blue as the dominant brand anchor.
- Use beige and gray to soften dense content.
- Avoid casual illustrations, playful icons, excessive gradients, and generic corporate stock aesthetics.
- Any photography should feel institutional, research-oriented, campus-related, or academically credible.

## Density Guidance

Do not leave slides visually underfilled.

- If the main content is short, add supporting context, key takeaway boxes, notes, source lines, compact diagrams, or related metrics.
- Fill the bottom area with useful information, not decoration.
- Maintain enough whitespace for readability, but avoid large unused blank zones.
- Use dense layouts only when they improve communication.
- Never overcrowd text to the point of reducing legibility.

## Do

- Use only 16:9 slides.
- Use Yoon Gothic for Korean and Univers LT Pro for English and numbers.
- Keep chapter name, title, and subtitle positions consistent.
- Place the SNU logo at the bottom-right, at least 10 mm wide and 10 mm tall.
- Place the 80th anniversary emblem at the top-right, slightly larger than the logo, preserving 16:9 ratio.
- Use SNU Blue as the main identity color.
- Use SNU Beige and SNU Gray for subtle contrast and structure.
- Keep slide bottoms meaningfully populated with useful content.
- Maintain a polished academic presentation style.

## Don't

- Do not use non-16:9 slide formats.
- Do not randomly reposition titles, subtitles, chapter names, logos, or emblems.
- Do not distort, recolor, crop, or rotate the SNU logo or 80th anniversary emblem.
- Do not make the logo smaller than 10 mm in either width or height.
- Do not leave the bottom third of slides empty without a clear design reason.
- Do not use playful fonts or decorative typefaces.
- Do not overuse bright colors outside the SNU palette.
- Do not create slides that feel like generic business templates disconnected from Seoul National University.

## Alignment Revision

Create a single 16:9 presentation slide using the Seoul National University visual identity.

### Object Alignment

- Every visible object should be placed on a disciplined grid.
- Each object group should use center alignment within its own container whenever the content is diagrammatic, card-based, or label-based.
- Text inside cards, diagram nodes, summary strips, and compact callout boxes should be vertically and horizontally centered unless a formal title/header block requires left alignment.
- Header elements may remain left-aligned for institutional hierarchy, but content blocks below the header should be centered within their respective shapes.
- Diagram nodes should share a common vertical centerline.
- Principle cards should have consistent width, height, baseline, and centered internal text.
- The bottom summary strip should center its contents vertically, with the label, summary sentence, and English keywords aligned to the same optical middle.

### Vertical Text Spacing

- Maintain approximately 1 mm vertical spacing between stacked text elements inside the same object.
- Avoid compressed or uneven line gaps.
- Avoid large accidental gaps between label, title, body, caption, and metadata inside a single card or node.
- Multi-line Korean body text should remain compact but legible, with consistent line spacing.
