# SNU PPT Skill

## Core Workflow

1. Create only 16:9 slides.
2. Read `references/snu_ppt_guidelines.md` before designing the deck or slide.
3. Use the bundled assets without recoloring, cropping, stretching, rotating, or adding effects:
   - Bottom-right SNU logo: `assets/snu_ui_download.png`
   - Top-right 80th anniversary emblem: `assets/Basic Horizontal with SNU Emblem KR Navy.png`
4. Use SNU Blue as the identity anchor, with white or very light backgrounds for most content slides.
5. Keep chapter label, slide title, subtitle, emblem, and logo positions consistent across slides.
6. Populate the lower slide region with meaningful content such as notes, sources, summary strips, compact callouts, or structured rows.
7. Center-align text inside cards, diagram nodes, summary strips, and compact callout boxes. Keep stacked text elements inside each object separated by approximately 1 mm.
8. Render or preview the deck when possible and correct obvious alignment, spacing, overflow, or logo placement issues before final delivery.

## Design Rules

- Keep the style institutional, precise, academic, and modern.
- Prefer dense-but-readable structured layouts over sparse single-line slides.
- Use disciplined grids; do not scatter objects freely.
- Use Yoon Gothic Pro for Korean text when available.
- Use Univers LT Pro for English, acronyms, numerals, dates, units, and chart labels when available.
- Use SNU Beige for subtle academic panels and SNU Gray for captions, metadata, table lines, and secondary text.
- Use SNU Gold and SNU Silver only as restrained premium accents.
- Avoid generic corporate templates, playful typography, saturated non-SNU colors, excessive gradients, and decorative effects.

## Asset Placement

- Place the SNU logo at the bottom-right whenever possible.
- Keep the SNU logo at least 10 mm wide and 10 mm tall in rendered size.
- Place the 80th anniversary emblem at the top-right where appropriate.
- Make the 80th anniversary emblem slightly larger than the bottom-right SNU logo.
- Preserve each asset's original proportions and clear space.

## Alignment Addendum

- Use center alignment inside each object group when the content is diagrammatic, card-based, or label-based.
- Use vertical and horizontal center alignment for text inside cards, diagram nodes, summary strips, and compact callout boxes.
- Allow formal page headers to remain left-aligned for academic hierarchy.
- Keep diagram nodes on a shared vertical centerline.
- Keep principle cards and repeated blocks equal in width, height, baseline, and internal alignment.
- Align bottom summary-strip contents to the same optical middle.
